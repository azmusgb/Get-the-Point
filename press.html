PROJECT OVERVIEW · SEPTEMBER 2026

DECISIONS, DECISIONS Puts the Choice Before the Clue

DECISIONS, DECISIONS, a new physical-and-digital social party game currently in development, is built around a deceptively simple idea: every prompt gives players multiple ways to communicate it, and the player chooses the risk before attempting the clue.

Rather than assigning one universal activity, DECISIONS, DECISIONS presents prompt-specific communication routes with different point values. The active player weighs the tradeoff, chooses a route, commits, performs, and teammates race to guess the prompt before the timer expires. Correct guesses earn the selected route's points.

The current prototype is testing a three-route challenger — HUM, DRAW, and MIME — along with a 60/30/15-second round structure. Those labels, timing rules, colors, icons, and visual details remain under active playtesting and should not be treated as final commercial specifications.

The project is being developed as both a compact physical game and an offline, pass-and-play digital experience. The physical prototype direction centers on a card deck, reusable drawing surface, quick-start rules, simple scoring, and a phone timer during development.

Content quality now includes a decision-tension test: a strong prompt should make at least two routes plausibly attractive once the point values are considered. The team tracks route selection, choice time, success/failure, guess time, dead-card behavior, rule questions, and replay interest.

The development strategy deliberately prioritizes blind playtesting before scaling manufacturing or software scope. The key milestone is not feature completeness; it is whether new groups can understand the game in roughly two minutes, make meaningful choices, laugh during play, and want another round.

DECISIONS, DECISIONS is also being developed with a content-driven expansion strategy. The core mechanic remains stable while future packs can introduce new prompt sets and visual personalities without fragmenting the game into separate mini-games.

DECISIONS, DECISIONS is the current lead working commercial title and remains subject to legal validation before commercial commitment.
