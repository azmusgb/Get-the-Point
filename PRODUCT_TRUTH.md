# DECISIONS, DECISIONS — Repository Product Truth v2.2

**Status date:** 2026-09-15  
**Current lead commercial working name:** **DECISIONS, DECISIONS**  
**Name status:** **CURRENT LEAD / PROPOSED / NEEDS LEGAL VALIDATION**

This repository mirror exists to stop historical prototype code and creative-direction artifacts from silently overriding the current product.

## 1. Core differentiator — LOCKED

**Choice under time pressure.** Every prompt gives the active player multiple communication routes with prompt-specific point values. The player chooses a route, commits, performs, and teammates guess before time expires.

The product must preserve:

**ONE PROMPT → MULTIPLE ROUTES → DIFFERENT RISK/REWARD → PLAYER CHOOSES → COMMIT → PERFORM → GUESS.**

Do not drift into generic Pictionary, charades, karaoke, trivia, or three separate mini-games.

## 2. Brand promise created by the title — CURRENT DIRECTION

**DECISIONS, DECISIONS names the repeated micro-moment at the center of play:**

> Which route is worth taking for this prompt, right now, for these points?

The title should not cause the interface or marketing copy to overuse the word “decision.” The name carries the attitude; gameplay copy should remain short and functional.

**Preferred functional language:**

- YOUR MOVE
- LOCKED IN
- CORRECT
- NEXT

**Primary descriptor:** **ONE PROMPT. THREE WAYS TO PLAY.**

**PROPOSED supporting line:** **CHOOSE YOUR WAY. LOCK IT IN.**

**PROPOSED mechanic explainer:** **See the prompt. Weigh the points. Pick a way. Make them guess.**

## 3. Product principles — LOCKED

- Choice before performance.
- Fast decisions and fast turns.
- Everybody participates.
- Failure should create comedy without humiliation.
- Content is the expansion engine.
- No subscription by default.
- New groups should begin in about two minutes.
- A card should create a real tradeoff, not a fake choice.

## 4. Current turn loop — LOCKED BASELINE

1. Reveal one prompt to the active player.
2. Show three communication methods and prompt-specific point values.
3. Player chooses one method.
4. Commitment becomes irreversible once clueing/performance begins.
5. Teammates guess while the timer runs.
6. Correct guess earns the selected points.
7. Advance while time remains.
8. Rotate player/team when the turn ends.

### Digital selection behavior

For the current digital prototype, **one tap selects and commits the route**. No second confirmation screen.

## 8. Imagery system — CURRENT DIRECTION

Marketing imagery should prove the mechanic rather than merely prove that people are having fun.

Priority sequence:

1. **Choice moment:** prompt + three methods + different points + visible hesitation/commitment.
2. **Performance:** the selected route in action.
3. **Guess/reaction:** team response and payoff.

Use generic party/lifestyle photography only when the product mechanic remains legible. The product, cards, phone UI, and choice surface should usually be the hero.

## 10. Content quality gate — LOCKED + NEW DECISION-TENSION TEST

Every core prompt must pass:

- Representability across all active methods.
- Clear/unambiguous target.
- Audience/cultural fit.
- Scoring balance based on observed difficulty.
- **Decision tension:** at least two routes should be plausibly attractive because of the point tradeoff.
- No accidental giveaway.
- Rights safety.

### Decision-tension test

Ask:

> Would a normal player plausibly pause between at least two methods because of the point tradeoff?

Track choice time, route selected, success/failure, guess time, dead-card rate, dominant-method behavior, rule questions, reaction, and replay interest. Remove weak prompts rather than inflating deck size.
